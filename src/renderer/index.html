    Get from game data to your first play in four steps. You can still browse stats without film.

    1. Choose your team and gameUse the selectors at the top.
    2. Choose a film folder or fileAMP22 never copies or changes your original video.
    3. Analyze onceA full game can take about 3–5 minutes; you can use Analysis Lab while it runs.
    4. Click a playUse the arrows or keyboard to move play by play.
